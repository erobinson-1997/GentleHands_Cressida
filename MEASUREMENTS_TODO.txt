CRESSIDA MX83 - REAL-CAR MEASUREMENTS FOR AC TUNING
====================================================
Gather these over time and give them to Claude to tune the mod.
Partial results are fine. Fill in the blanks as you go.

Priority if short on time: #1, #2, #5 (cheap, quick, and they replace the
values Claude is least sure about).


1. DIFF BREAKAWAY TORQUE  (10 min, torque wrench)
-------------------------------------------------
  - Jack up BOTH rear wheels. Car in neutral, handbrake off.
  - Spin one rear wheel by hand:
      other wheel turns the SAME direction     -> LSD
      other wheel turns the OPPOSITE direction -> open diff
  - If LSD: put a torque wrench on one lug nut while the other wheel is held
    still. Note the reading when the wheel starts to turn.
  -> goes straight into PRELOAD in drivetrain.ini

  Diff type (LSD/open): ________
  Breakaway torque:     ________ ft-lb / Nm


2. ACCELERATION PULL  (phone GPS app: Dragy, Harry's LapTimer, RaceChrono)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Full-throttle 3rd-gear pull from 30 to 70 mph on a flat road.
  - Run it in BOTH directions and average them (cancels wind and slope).
  - Also note the RPM at 60 mph in 5th, read off the tach
    (checks gearing and tyre size).
  -> Claude matches the same pull in AC and adjusts power.lut
  -> A dyno sheet beats all of this, if you ever get one.

  3rd gear 30-70 time, direction A: ________ s
  3rd gear 30-70 time, direction B: ________ s
  RPM at 60 mph in 5th:             ________


3. COAST-DOWN TEST  (aero drag + rolling resistance)
----------------------------------------------------
  - Flat, empty road, no wind. Get to 70 mph, shift to neutral, time how
    long it takes to coast down to 40 mph.
  - Do it in BOTH directions.
  -> tells Claude whether the top end feels strong because of the engine
     or because the car's air resistance is set too low (aero.ini)

  70->40 coast time, direction A: ________ s
  70->40 coast time, direction B: ________ s


4. SUSPENSION LEVERAGE  (jack + tape measure)
---------------------------------------------
  The rear wheel rate in the mod (35,000 N/m) is a GUESS. Measuring this
  replaces the guess with a real number.
  - Remove the spring, or loosen the coilover collar until it's slack.
  - Jack under the hub/knuckle and measure the shock length (top mount to
    bottom mount) at two hub heights about 25 mm apart.
  - Leverage ratio = change in shock length / change in hub height.

                      Hub height   Shock length
  FRONT position 1:   ________     ________
  FRONT position 2:   ________     ________
  REAR  position 1:   ________     ________
  REAR  position 2:   ________     ________


5. ALIGNMENT SHEET
------------------
  - The printout from your last alignment: camber, caster and toe in
    degrees for all four corners. A photo is fine.

  Got it? (y/n): ____   Saved where: ______________________________


6. WEIGHT
---------
  - BEST: corner weights (alignment shops, race shops, or track days often
    have scales).
  - ALTERNATIVE: a truck-stop CAT scale. Weigh the front axle and rear axle
    separately, then the whole car.
  - Note how much fuel was in the tank and whether you were in the car.

  Corner weights   LF: ______  RF: ______  LR: ______  RR: ______
  OR  Front axle: ______  Rear axle: ______  Total: ______
  Fuel level: ______   Driver in car (y/n): ____


7. STATIC MEASUREMENTS
----------------------
  - Ride height at all 4 corners: centre of the hub to the fender lip,
    car on the ground.
  - Sway bar diameters front and rear (calipers). Stock or aftermarket?
  - BC damper settings: clicks counted from full stiff.
  - Steering:
      steering-wheel turns lock to lock
      wheel angle at full lock (turn plates at an alignment shop), OR
      turning circle curb-to-curb in a parking lot

  Ride height   LF: ______  RF: ______  LR: ______  RR: ______
  Sway bar      Front: ______ mm  Rear: ______ mm   Stock? ____
  BC clicks from full stiff   Front: ______  Rear: ______
  Lock-to-lock turns: ______
  Wheel angle at full lock: ______  OR  Turning circle: ______


8. TYRES
--------
  - Hot pressures: check right after 15 minutes of hard driving.
    -> sets PRESSURE_IDEAL (the pressure each tyre works best at)
  - The 351's UTQG rating off the sidewall (e.g. "TREADWEAR 200 A A")
    and its exact size.
  - OPTIONAL: lateral g on a steady circle in an empty lot, using a
    g-meter app with the phone mounted flat. One run on the PHI, one on
    the 351.
    -> lets Claude set the grip difference from real numbers

  Hot pressures, PHI    LF: ____  RF: ____  LR: ____  RR: ____
  Hot pressures, 351    LF: ____  RF: ____  LR: ____  RR: ____
  351 UTQG: ______________   Size: ______________
  Steady-circle lateral g    PHI: ______   351: ______
